ShadowBitmapDescriptorFactory

@Implements(value = BitmapDescriptorFactory::class)
open class ShadowBitmapDescriptorFactory

Robolectric shadow for BitmapDescriptorFactory.

In production runtime environments, BitmapDescriptorFactory communicates with the Google Play Services Maps module to construct native bitmap representations. In local unit tests without Play Services initialized, direct invocations throw a NullPointerException.

This shadow intercepts all factory methods and creates valid BitmapDescriptor instances that retain references to their underlying bitmaps, assets, or identifiers for headless assertions and rendering.
